## 💡 Filming Tips to Nail This Exact Script

1. **Splitting the Audio/Video for the Final Shot:**
   - Record the first 4 paragraphs in your office lab.
   - For the final line (*"With enough liquidity, we can tokenize anything..."*), shoot it in the field near the wind turbines.
   - You can speak the entire final sentence directly to camera in the field, or deliver the first half in the office, and cut outdoors right as you say *"With enough liquidity..."* so the visual punch lands right on *"or even these turbines!"*.
2. **Delivery Tone on the Trees:**
   - When you say *"as you can see, there's actually a lot of moving parts to the trees. So from the perspective of yield, they will not always be the most interesting product"*, deliver it with an authentic, grounded founder smile. Investors and judges LOVE founders who are intellectually honest about hardware complexity vs. financial yield. It proves you understand unit economics.
3. **Sound Mixing for the Drone Climax:**
   - In the office, keep the background music low and rhythmic.
   - When you cut to the field, add a subtle wind sweep and let the music build into a powerful synth hit as the drone reveals the turbines.
